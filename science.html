Interactive Space Presentation & DIY Mini RC Car Project

🛠️ Required Components (सामग्री):

  • Small DC Toy Motor (12V or 5V)
  • Plastic Toy Wheels (4x) & Axles
  • Cardboard / Plastic Chassis Base
  • 9V Battery & Battery Connector Clip
  • OnOff Switch & Connecting Wires

🏎️ How to Make Mini RC Car (स्टेप्स):

STEP 01

Chassis Frame Base Banayein

Sabse pehle lightweight Cardboard ya Plastic sheet ko car ki body ke shape me cut karein. Yeh aapki car ka main base (chassis) hoga jispar motor aur wheels fit honge.

STEP 02

Wheels & Axle Fit Karein

Chassis ke niche wale hiss par Plastic Straws lagakar unke andar Axle rod daalein aur dono taraf wheels ko connect karein taaki car aasaani se roll kar sake.

STEP 03

DC Motor Attachment

Peechhe wale wheels (Rear Axle) ke saath DC Motor ko gear ya rubber band ke zariye connect karein taaki motor ghoomne par gaadi aage badh sake.

STEP 04

Circuit Connection & Testing

Switch, Battery connector clip, aur Motor ko wires ke zariye series me solder/connect karein. Battery lagakar Switch ON karein aur apni Mini Science Car test karein!
